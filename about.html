About Us

The Project

TREVOC Royal Residences is a landmark luxury development on Golf Course Road, Sector 56, Gurugram — one of the city’s most established and coveted addresses. Conceived as twin wave-inspired towers, it brings together architecture, landscape and hospitality in a single, rare composition.

  • A limited edition of 172 residences — 3 & 4 BHK luxe homes, with the first residential level rising above 80 feet.
  • A striking façade with a dynamic wave design at the podium and aluminium-perforated detailing.
  • A Singapore-inspired vertical green landscape by Coopers Hill, woven across three levels — Ground, Fourth and Skydeck.
  • A three-level Club Royal and a rooftop Skydeck with an amphitheatre, jogging track and 360° observation decks.
All project details are indicative and governed by the developer’s RERA-registered disclosures. Please read the Disclaimer.
